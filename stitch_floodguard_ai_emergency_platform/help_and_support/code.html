How FloodGuard AI Works

Plain-English answers on how we keep you ahead of rising water.

We combine live IMD rainfall forecasts, Khadakwasla & Panshet dam release updates, and street-level water sensors. When water levels rise near your saved zones, you get a calm, early warning with clear next steps.

FloodGuard AI maps elevation and known low-lying underpasses across Pune. If a bridge or road is submerged, your route automatically shifts to higher ground and verified relief shelters.

Your last-downloaded Pune safe-zone map, emergency helplines, and shelter locations stay saved on your phone. Even without internet, you can still view nearby shelters and dial 112 or 1077.
